about

I am a PhD student in the Goncalves Lab at KU Leuven, affiliated with the VIB Center for AI and Computational Biology. Before starting my PhD, I worked as a researcher at Neuro-Electronics Research Flanders (NERF), the UMN Center for Magnetic Resonance Research, and the UMN Center for Cognitive Sciences. I hold a B.S. in Computer Science from the University of Minnesota.

research

My research focuses on simulation-based inference and Bayesian methods for modeling perceptual decision-making, with applications spanning computational neuroscience and machine learning.
